05 / CAMPAIGN & ADVOCACY DESIGN

Safe Spaces Advocates — Prevention & Response Toolkit

A campaign identity and practical toolkit for Safe Spaces Advocates, designed to turn campus awareness into action at Obafemi Awolowo University. The toolkit breaks down what consent looks like in real-life situations, outlines five practical steps bystanders can take to intervene safely, and lists emergency contacts students can reach for immediate support — built to read clearly as a trifold handout and hold up printed at scale.

Rollout in two stages: campaign posters first carried the message from the hostels to the lecture areas across campus, then the toolkit moved into full physical implementation — printed and placed directly into students' hands — alongside a large outdoor campaign banner to keep the conversation visible. Supported by the LEAP Africa Youth Leadership Development Programme and the Nigeria Youth Futures Fund (NYFF).
RoleGraphic Designer — Campaign Toolkit & Field Rollout
DateSafe Spaces Advocates Campaign · OAU, Nigeria · Sept 2026

Faces of students and bystanders in field photography have been intentionally obscured to protect their privacy.
